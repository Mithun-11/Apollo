# Surface: Apollo app (listen, answer, class mode)

Scope: frontend/app (listening screen, result, post-match replay, class mode). Mode: Experience
(the class is inside the work; the recording itself leads). Normal use keeps a short highlight;
Class mode is a presenter-stepped replay (arrow keys / clicker) of every pipeline stage built
from the recording just made. Audience: a signals class and a teacher who has not studied the
project, on a classroom projector (1920x1080, 1366x768). Constraint: recognition speed is
untouched: the answer shows on the first confirmed live check, /recognize/evidence runs only
when the replay is opened, and nothing heavy renders while listening (AGENTS.md, "Speed comes
first").

Brief-pinned aesthetic (user, verbatim intent): anime-film inspired like Studio Ghibli and
Makoto Shinkai's Your Name; a masterpiece, not gradient AI slop. This pin overrides the rolled
direction (seed da5f014a assigned "Visible Speech"); its registration/stamp/damped-pen raises
survive as behaviour, translated into the pinned world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The recording is a twilight sky. Its spectrogram is painted as the sky itself (time
across, pitch up), its peaks are the stars, and a match is musubi: red braided threads tying each
of your stars to its twin in the song across the gap in time. Refuses the category default: a
dark app with a glowing button and bar visualizer.

OWN-WORLD: Painted kataware-doki dusk: deep ultramarine #16204a to violet #3b2f6b sky with
cel-shaded cloud bands and film grain, a low rose-gold horizon #f0a47a over a still lake that
mirrors the sky, star white #fff6e6, kumihimo red #e2323f for threads and the verdict only, comet
teal #9fe3e0 for the catalog song. Hand-inked hairlines, no UI gradients, no glass. Type:
Shippori Mincho B1 for film-title display, Zen Kaku Gothic New for UI and figures (tabular).

STORY: The class watches their own sound become a sky, learns each step as one sentence and one
real number, sees why noise cannot break the stars, watches the song's sky slide until every
thread pulls taut, and leaves able to explain fingerprinting, edits, and covers. They trust the
answer because every shape was computed from the recording.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed painted dusk. The title "Apollo" set small and vertical like a film
credit at the left edge; centre-low a single ink-drawn record control on the lake line (source
switch beside it). While listening, the sky paints in live from the microphone, stars ignite
where peaks form, and the horizon line is the live waveform. The answer arrives as a film title
card over the sky.

FORM: Brief-pinned anime twilight world (outside the dealt hand), seed key da5f014a; signature
interaction: the musubi lock (song sky slides in 3D until threads tighten and a light pillar
rises from the lake at the winning offset). Motion grammar: slow camera drift, stars ignite with
a soft bloom, threads pull taut with damped spring, verdict lands once.
